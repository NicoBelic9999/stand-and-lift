# Stand & Lift

Standing, straight-back exercises with a dumbbell in each hand or two 10 kg plates.

- 89 exercises, each with a how-to video
- No jumping, no lunges, no squats, no bending
- Every exercise: at least 4 sets
- Filters by muscle, a Plates filter, pinned favourites and a random-exercise button

## Use it
Open `index.html` in any browser. It works offline (videos need internet).

## Android app
Download [apk/Stand-and-Lift.apk](apk/Stand-and-Lift.apk) and open it on your phone to install.
